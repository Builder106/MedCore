# Med-Core Roadmap

Medical data pipeline and healthcare data modeling platform roadmap.

## v1.1 — FHIR / HL7 Data Normalization

- **Healthcare Record Parser**: Robust normalization for clinical records and synthetic patient datasets.
- **Data Privacy & De-Identification**: Deterministic HIPAA-compliant anonymization pipeline.

## v1.2 — Clinical Analytics & Risk Scoring

- **Risk Stratification Engine**: Rule-based and statistical health index calculations.
- **Validation Harness**: Automated test suites against standardized medical ontology benchmarks (SNOMED, LOINC).

## Out of Scope

- Direct clinical diagnosis without human physician review
- Live unprotected PHI storage without dedicated HSM encryption
